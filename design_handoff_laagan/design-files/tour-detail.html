Santa Cruz Island Tour

⏱ 8 hours 👥 2–20 guests 📍 Paseo del Mar Jetty ⭐ 5.0 (124 reviews) 🏝 Island Tour
🔥 Most Popular Only 400 visitors/day on Santa Cruz Island Weekends sell out fast

Overview

You'll spend a full day on one of the most extraordinary beaches in the world — a beach with naturally pink sand, crystal-clear water, and a jellyfish lagoon where you can swim freely. We sail there on a traditional Tausug vinta: colorful, hand-crafted outrigger boats that have crossed these waters for centuries.

🏖
The only pink sand beach in Asia — National Geographic Top 21 Beaches in the World (2017)
🪼
Swim with stingless jellyfish — one of only a few places on earth where this is safely possible
⛵
Sail on a traditional vinta — authentic Tausug outrigger boats, unique to Zamboanga

Full Day Itinerary

Gather at the Paseo del Mar waterfront. James or a guide will meet your group, brief you on safety, and help you board the vinta. Tip: Arrive 10 minutes early to get a good spot.
A 60-minute sail through Zamboanga waters on a traditional vinta. Perfect for photos — the colorful sails against the ocean are unforgettable.
Free time on the pink sand beach. Swim, snorkel the coral gardens, visit the stingless jellyfish lagoon, and explore the sandbar. Maximum 400 visitors are admitted per day — it stays uncrowded.
Packed Filipino lunch served under the shade. Rice, viand, fresh buko (coconut) water. Vegetarian options available — let us know when booking.
Depart from Santa Cruz Island. Speedboat return trip for efficiency. Back at Paseo del Mar by 3:00–3:30 PM depending on conditions.
