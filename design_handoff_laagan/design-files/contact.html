Common Questions

Before you reach out

Do I need to pay upfront?
No. You book, we confirm, you pay on the day in cash or GCash. We only ask for a ₱300 deposit via GCash to reserve slots for large groups.
What if the weather is bad?
Your safety is our priority. We reschedule at no charge due to weather. You will never lose your money because of something outside your control.
How far in advance should I book?
Santa Cruz Island only allows 400 visitors/day. For weekends and holidays, book 1–2 weeks ahead. Weekday tours often have same-day availability.
